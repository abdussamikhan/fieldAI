[00:00:00] Lead Auditor: Thank you everyone for joining today's walkthrough of the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process for Alpha Corp. I have with me Sarah from Operations and Tariq from Procurement. Today we want to understand how purchase requisitions are created, approved, and matched to vendor invoices.
[00:00:45] Sarah (Operations): Hello. When any department needs items or services, the requisitioner enters a Purchase Requisition (PR) in SAP. If it is under $10,000, it routes to me for approval. Over $10,000, it requires Division VP approval, and over $50,000 it must go to the CFO. The system automatically verifies that the department has sufficient budget remaining before allowing submission.
[00:02:15] Tariq (Procurement): Once Sarah approves the PR, it enters our procurement work queue. We verify the item category and select a vendor from our approved vendor master. For purchases over $50,000, our policy requires three competitive quotations. After selecting the vendor, we generate the Purchase Order (PO) in SAP.
[00:04:10] Lead Auditor: How do you verify vendor bank details when creating or updating the vendor master record?
[00:04:30] Tariq (Procurement): Any change to vendor bank details requires an official letter on vendor letterhead signed by their authorized signatory, plus an independent telephone callback to the verified company registry number before Finance updates the record.
[00:06:00] Sarah (Operations): When physical goods arrive at the central warehouse, the warehouse team checks the physical count against the delivery note and logs the Goods Receipt Note (GRN) in SAP within 24 hours.
[00:08:15] Tariq (Procurement): When Accounts Payable receives the invoice, SAP performs a strict 3-way match between the PO, GRN, and Invoice. If the price or quantity differs by more than zero percent, payment is automatically blocked.
