STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PROCURE-TO-PAY (P2P)
Document ID: SOP-FIN-04
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 1 January 2024
Owner: Financial Controller
Classification: Internal Audit Approved

1. OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
This document outlines the mandatory controls and procedures for ordering, receiving, and settling liabilities for goods and services across Alpha Corp.

2. PURCHASE REQUISITIONS (PR)
2.1 All business units must enter requisitions electronically via SAP ERP.
2.2 The system performs an automated hard-budget availability check. If insufficient budget exists, the PR is automatically rejected.

3. DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY & APPROVAL LIMITS
3.1 Requisitions up to $10,000: Approved by Department Head.
3.2 Requisitions $10,001 to $50,000: Approved by Division Vice President.
3.3 Requisitions exceeding $50,000: Approved by Chief Financial Officer (CFO). Minimum of three (3) independent competitive quotations must be attached.

4. GOODS RECEIPT (GRN)
4.1 Physical goods received at the central logistics warehouse must undergo count and physical damage inspection.
4.2 A Goods Receipt Note (GRN) must be recorded in SAP within 24 hours of delivery before any invoice can be scheduled for payment.

5. THREE-WAY MATCHING & DISBURSEMENT
5.1 Accounts Payable performs automated 3-way matching in SAP across Purchase Order, Goods Receipt Note, and Supplier Invoice.
5.2 Tolerance for price variance is 0%. Any price deviation requires formal Procurement Manager override memo.
5.3 Payment disbursements are processed weekly via dual-authorized electronic funds transfer.
